## About Bad Time Simulator

Bad Time Simulator is a browser-based gaming hub dedicated to Undertale, Deltarune, and their fan-made battle communities.

The website brings together a wide variety of fight simulators, alternate-universe encounters, boss challenges, and community-created battle experiences. Whether you want to practice the classic Sans fight, discover a different interpretation of a familiar character, or test your reflexes against a difficult fan-made boss, the collection provides a convenient place to explore.

Our goal is to make these games easier to discover, understand, and play. Alongside browser-playable games, the website provides gameplay instructions, control information, descriptions, and recommendations for related battles.

The original Bad Time Simulator experience is based on the Sans fight recreation developed by jcw87. Other games featured in the collection come from various independent fan-game creators.

## How to Play Bad Time Simulator

The classic Sans fight combines turn-based menu decisions with real-time bullet-dodging mechanics.

During attacks, you control a small heart known as the SOUL. Your objective is to survive increasingly complex attack patterns by responding to the position, movement, and timing of incoming obstacles.

### Keyboard Controls

| Key | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow Keys | Move the SOUL and navigate menus |
| WASD | Alternative movement controls |
| Z / Enter | Confirm selections and advance dialogue |
| X / Shift | Cancel or return to the previous menu |

A keyboard is recommended for the classic Sans battle. Controls may differ between individual fan-made games, and touchscreen support depends on the game.

**Tip:** If keyboard input stops working, click inside the game window to restore focus.

### Battle Modes

The classic simulator includes several modes designed for different play styles:

- **Normal Mode:** Attempt the complete Sans boss encounter.
- **Practice Mode:** Learn difficult attack patterns with recovery after failed attempts.
- **Endless Mode:** Test your endurance against repeated attacks.
- **Single Attack Mode:** Practice individual patterns without replaying the entire encounter.
- **Custom Attack Mode:** Load compatible custom attack scripts to experiment with different battle patterns.

These modes apply to the classic simulator. Other fan-made games may offer different mechanics or options.

### Beginner Tips

1. Start with Practice Mode before attempting a complete run.
2. Use short, controlled movements instead of holding directional keys continuously.
3. Learn the difference between white bones and blue bones: blue attacks generally require you to remain stationary to avoid damage.
4. Watch for changes in gravity when the SOUL turns blue.
5. Practice difficult sequences individually before returning to Normal Mode.

Improvement comes from recognizing patterns, controlling movement, and developing consistent timing rather than relying on reflexes alone.

### Troubleshooting and Compatibility

- **Keyboard input is not responding:** Click inside the game window to give it focus, then try the controls described on the game page.
- **A battle is taking too long to load:** Allow the game to initialize. If it remains stuck, refresh the page and check whether browser extensions are interfering.
- **The controls feel different:** Fan-made games can have different keyboard mappings, modes, and interaction patterns; follow each game's instructions.
- **Playing on mobile:** Touchscreen support varies across games. Use a keyboard when a fight depends on precise movement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Bad Time Simulator free to play?

The games in the browser collection can be accessed without purchasing a game package. Individual experiences run directly through their respective game pages.

### Do I need to install anything?

The website is designed for browser-based play. The Chrome extension is an optional alternative for offline Sans fight practice.

### Can I play on mobile?

Some games support touchscreen controls, but compatibility varies. A desktop or laptop with a keyboard is recommended for games requiring precise movement.

### Are all games developed by the same creator?

No. The collection brings together fan-made experiences associated with different creators and projects. The inclusion of a game does not imply that Bad Time Simulator created the original game.

### Is the complete Undertale game included?

No. This collection focuses on individual battle simulators and fan-made encounters rather than distributing the complete original Undertale or Deltarune games.
